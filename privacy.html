Privacy Policy

MDitor collects nothing.

Effective September 29, 2026

What MDitor collects

Nothing. MDitor has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting service and no servers of its own. The developer never receives your notes, your files or any information about how you use the app.

Your notes

Your notes are plain files in the folders you choose. MDitor reads and writes them only on your Mac. If those folders are synced by a service such as iCloud Drive, Dropbox or Google Drive, that service's own privacy policy applies to the synced files.

To remember your folders, window positions, pinned notes and search index, MDitor keeps a small database inside its own sandboxed container on your Mac. It never leaves your Mac, and deleting the app deletes it.

Internet access

MDitor connects to the internet for one purpose only: to download a picture you pasted from a web page, or a picture you explicitly asked it to download into a note. The request goes directly to the website that hosts the picture; no cookies are sent or stored. Opening, reading or saving a note never connects to the internet.

Access to your files

MDitor runs in the macOS App Sandbox. It can only open the folders and files you choose, and you can revoke access at any time by closing the folder in MDitor or removing it from the sidebar.

Children

MDitor collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
