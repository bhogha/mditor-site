Common questions

Where are my notes stored?

In the notes folder you chose when you first opened MDitor, as ordinary .md files. Notes you haven't saved to a folder yet are drafts; press ⌘S to give one a name and a place.

Can I sync my notes between Macs?

Yes. Choose a folder inside iCloud Drive, Dropbox or Google Drive as your notes folder. MDitor notices when another Mac changes a note, and if both changed it at once, it keeps both versions.

Where do pictures go?

Into an assets folder next to the note, so the note and its pictures travel together.

A folder shows as "Unreachable".

The folder was moved, renamed or deleted, or is on a drive that isn't connected. Choose Choose Again… to point MDitor at it, or Remove to stop listing it.

Can MDitor open Markdown written by other apps?

Yes — Obsidian vaults, blog posts, READMEs. Anything MDitor doesn't display is kept exactly as it was: open a file, change nothing, save, and not a byte is different.
